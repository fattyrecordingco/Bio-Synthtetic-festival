---
name: frontend-copy-cleanup
description: Edit visible website or app copy to remove generic AI phrasing, inflated claims and redundant instructions while preserving useful actions, factual qualifications and accessibility labels. Use for requested interface copy cleanup, not for rewriting essays or programme notes.
---

Read the rendered interface and its content source. Judge each phrase by what a visitor needs to understand or do at that point.

Remove empty invitations, repeated explanations, abstract technology metaphors and promotional claims that could describe any product. Prefer specific names, times, places and actions. Keep one useful instruction at the relevant control instead of repeating it across a section.

Use short verbs for actions. An icon may replace a familiar visible control such as play, pause, close or download when it has an accessible name, keyboard focus and an adequate hit area. Keep text for unfamiliar actions, navigation destinations and factual distinctions that an icon cannot communicate reliably. Do not replace essential labels with tooltips alone.

Preserve the user's voice, artwork text, supplied quotations, protected long-form writing, attribution and distinctions between proposals and existing work. Do not silently remove uncertainty, word-count constraints or recording credits to make the page look cleaner. Do not invent claims to fill the resulting space.

After editing, inspect the page at mobile and desktop sizes. Check that each action still says what it does, icon buttons have accessible names and changed labels match their states. Report concrete changes briefly; avoid presenting a speculative jargon score as a measurement.
