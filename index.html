BECA / the biosynth by Anshul Jain / adotj
Leaf, hand,
circuit.
Scale. Root note. Tempo. The performer sets a framework; incoming plant data supplies generative material. Octave range, sensitivity and rhythmic settings shape what can unfold.
In his solo, adotj uses synthesizers and MIDI controllers to shape the sound live. Other artists bring their own instruments and systems.
